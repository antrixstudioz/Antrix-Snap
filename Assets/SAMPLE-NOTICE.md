# Sample provenance

The 29 recordings were supplied by the project owner in SNAPS REF.rar. The archive included filenames labelled Cymatics and KSHMR, alongside other snap recordings. Internal identifiers have been renamed Snap_001 through Snap_029. These names do not transfer ownership or replace the source recordings’ licenses. The repository does not grant redistribution rights to those recordings.

The original archive is retained locally, outside the source repository. One source (Trill Snap.wav) ended with an incomplete PCM byte; the decoder recovered complete audio frames and discarded that trailing byte. All 29 recordings were decoded into the embedded bank.
